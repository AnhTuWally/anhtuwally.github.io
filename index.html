About

Bridging mathematical rigor and production engineering

My work sits at the intersection of formal problem-solving and practical production software. Trained as a mathematician and working across technical roles as a generalist, I have extensive experience developing in Python and JavaScript — designing automation with simplification and ease of maintenance in mind so teams get dependable tools without unnecessary complexity.

Over five-plus years in animation pipeline development, I have focused on reliably moving data of different sizes, shapes, and formats across software, users, and environments. From artist-facing DCC tools to automated asset and scene handoffs, I enjoy turning messy real-world bottlenecks into clean, composable workflows.

Alongside software and pipeline engineering, I bring hands-on IT infrastructure experience, including managing Proxmox clusters and firewall management using pfSense — keeping the underlying compute, networking, and security systems reliable for creative and engineering teams.
